Nusa Penida

Getting to the island and around it.

Nusa Penida is a 30 to 40 minute fast boat ride from Sanur Harbor in East Denpasar to either Toyapakeh or Sampalan Harbor. We arrange the whole journey door to door: a private car from Bali's airport or your hotel, fast boat tickets, luggage handling at both harbors, and the 15 to 20 minute drive up the hill to our check-in desk.

The resort sits at Ped on the north coast, 5 km (15 to 20 minutes) from Nusa Penida Harbor. Kelingking Beach is about 18 km (45 to 60 minutes), Angel's Billabong and Broken Beach about 17 km (45 to 60 minutes), and Diamond Beach about 25 km (60 to 75 minutes). Ngurah Rai Airport in Bali is roughly 65 km away, about 3 hours including the fast boat.

Activities

Tours and trips our concierge arranges.

Yes. We offer curated half-day and full-day private tours with a professional driver-guide to Kelingking Beach, Angel's Billabong, Broken Beach, Diamond Beach, Atuh Beach and the Thousand Islands viewpoint. Ask the concierge the day before and we will confirm the departure time.
