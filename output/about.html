A New Hilltop Retreat in Paradise

A Refined Island Sanctuary

Where elevated luxury meets the ocean panorama

Perched on the tranquil hills of Nusa Penida, The Sankara Hill Penida is a refined island sanctuary where elevated luxury meets breathtaking ocean panoramas. Thoughtfully designed to harmonize with the surrounding landscape, it blends contemporary tropical architecture with subtle Balinese touches, offering a serene escape defined by comfort, privacy, and authentic island charm.

The resort features 41 elegantly appointed accommodations, comprising stylish suites and exclusive private pool villas overlooking the hills and ocean. Complemented by an infinity pool, curated dining experiences, wellness facilities, and personalized hospitality, The Sankara Hill Penida invites guests to embrace the beauty and tranquility of Nusa Penida through an unforgettable island escape.

Nestled on the hills of Nusa Penida, the resort is designed for travelers seeking comfort, privacy, and unforgettable moments: a sanctuary of serenity where modern luxury meets the view.

The Names We Live By

Four Names, One Hill

Each venue on the hill carries a Pali or Sanskrit name. The meaning is explained once, here, and then simply lived.

Dining

Puṇṇa

Fullness, completeness, quiet abundance

Puṇṇa (Pali: पुण्ण) evokes fullness, completeness, abundance, and fulfillment. Closely associated with the imagery of the full moon, it reflects a sense of wholeness and quiet abundance. The name draws inspiration from the luminous full moon (Puṇṇacanda) that illuminates the limestone hills and surrounding ocean of Nusa Penida.

Puṇṇa Restaurant
Pool & Sunset Deck

Sukha

A state of ease

Sukha (Pali: सुख) means happiness, comfort, ease, and well-being, the opposite of Dukkha, or suffering. The name captures the essence of the experience: swim, relax, sip, take in the view, and watch the sunset. A simple state of being where the body feels light and the mind can simply be at ease.

Sukha Pool & Sunset Deck
Spa

Radha

The sky-ridge sanctuary

Radha (Sanskrit: राधा) is traditionally known as the beloved of Krishna, symbolizing devotion, love, and spiritual connection. The name represents a sanctuary of well-being where weary souls can pause, reconnect, and find a deeper sense of peace, high above the island, embraced by the serenity of Nusa Penida.

Radha Spa Hill Penida
Weddings

Archapala

Guardian of the sacred

Archapala is a contemporary name inspired by the Sanskrit roots Arcā, meaning reverence and sacred worship, and Pāla, meaning guardian or protector. Together, the name evokes a guardian of the sacred: a place where love, devotion, and lifelong vows are held in reverence.

Archapala Wedding Chapel
