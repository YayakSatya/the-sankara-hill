Facilities Overview

Refined Comfort, Hilltop Setting

Arrival

Sankara Hill Penida Lobby

A warm yet sophisticated arrival experience, where contemporary design meets panoramic views of the surrounding hills and ocean, creating a welcoming sense of place.

Dining

Puṇṇa Restaurant

Refined all-day dining overlooking the picturesque hills and ocean of Nusa Penida. Elegant indoor and alfresco seating present a curated selection of Indonesian and international Mediterranean cuisine, crafted from fresh, locally sourced ingredients — with in-room & in-villa dining also available.

Wellness & Leisure

Infinity Pool, Gym & Yoga Pavilion

Overlooking the vast ocean horizon, the infinity pool offers a breathtaking setting to unwind and embrace unforgettable sunsets. A modern gym and an open-air yoga pavilion complete the wellness routine.

Spa

Radha Spa Hill Penida

A tranquil wellness sanctuary offering holistic treatments inspired by Balinese healing traditions. Guided by experienced therapists, guests are invited to restore balance and relaxation in a serene hilltop setting.
