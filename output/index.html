Welcome to The Sankara Hill Penida

The Altitude’s Serenade

Perched upon the tranquil hills of Nusa Penida, The Sankara Hill Penida is a refined island sanctuary where elevated comfort meets sweeping ocean panoramas. Thoughtfully designed to harmonise with its natural surroundings, the resort blends contemporary tropical architecture with subtle Balinese touches, creating a serene retreat defined by comfort, privacy, and authentic island charm.

The resort features 41 elegantly appointed accommodations, comprising stylish suites and private pool villas overlooking the hills and ocean. An infinity pool, curated dining experiences, wellness facilities, and attentive hospitality complete the experience, inviting guests to slow down, reconnect, and embrace the beauty and tranquillity of Nusa Penida.

At The Sankara Hill Penida, the island unfolds from a different perspective — above, beyond, and at your own pace.

About the Name

The Sankara

A Name That Calls the Soul Home

The name Sankara is rooted in Sanskrit, the ancient language of India and a profound influence on the spiritual traditions of Hinduism and Buddhism. Traditionally associated with auspiciousness, benevolence, and positive transformation, Sankara evokes the idea of one who brings forth happiness and well-being.

The name also recalls the sacred sound of the conch shell trumpet, traditionally used in Hindu ceremonies and described in the Mahabharata as a powerful call marking significant moments. Its resonant sound continues to carry spiritual meaning, symbolising purity, sanctity, and a connection to the divine.

At The Sankara, this symbolism is reinterpreted as a gentle call to return to what matters most — to nature, to cultural roots, and to the stillness within. It is a journey back to the source: a place where the rhythm of nature, the richness of tradition, and the beauty of the island come together to inspire peace, balance, and a renewed sense of connection.

The Sankara — where every journey brings you closer to the source.

Dining

Puñña Restaurant

The Sacred Moonlight Dining of Nusa Penida

Puñña (Pali: पुञ्ञ) evokes fullness, completeness, abundance, and fulfillment. Closely associated with the imagery of the full moon, it reflects a sense of wholeness and quiet abundance. The name draws inspiration from the luminous full moon (Puññacanda) that illuminates the limestone hills and surrounding ocean of Nusa Penida. Puñña Restaurant celebrates the island through a refined dining experience.

Designed for refined all-day dining overlooking the picturesque hills and ocean of Nusa Penida, Puñña offers elegant indoor and alfresco seating. The restaurant presents a thoughtfully curated selection of Indonesian and international Mediterranean cuisines, crafted from fresh, locally sourced ingredients to deliver a memorable culinary experience from breakfast through dinner.

Open daily 10.00 – 22.00
Pool & Sunset Deck

Sukha Pool & Sunset Deck

A State of Ease

Sip on an array of refreshing beverages and handcrafted cocktails while basking in the island sun or unwinding after a day of exploration. Adjacent to Puñña Restaurant and the infinity pool, Sukha Pool & Deck offers a relaxed yet sophisticated setting overlooking the ocean horizon, perfect for leisurely afternoons and captivating sunsets.

Sukha (Pali: सुख) means happiness, comfort, ease, and well-being — the opposite of Dukkha, or suffering. The name captures the essence of the experience: swim, relax, sip, take in the view, and watch the sunset — a simple state of being where the body feels light and the mind can simply be at ease.

Open daily 11.00 – 22.00
Wellness

Radha Spa Hill Penida

The Sky-Ridge Sanctuary of Nusa Penida

Radha (Sanskrit: राधा) is traditionally known as the beloved of Krishna, symbolizing devotion, love, and spiritual connection. The name represents a sanctuary of well-being where weary souls can pause, reconnect, and find a deeper sense of peace — high above the island, embraced by the serenity of Nusa Penida.

A tranquil wellness sanctuary offering holistic treatments inspired by Balinese healing traditions. Guided by experienced therapists, guests are invited to restore balance and relaxation in a serene hilltop setting.
