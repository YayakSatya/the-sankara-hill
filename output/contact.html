Getting Here

From Bali to the Hill

Nusa Penida is a 30 to 40 minute fast boat ride from Sanur. Share your flight details and we arrange every leg of the journey.

  1. 1

    Arrive in Bali

    A private car collects you from Ngurah Rai Airport or your hotel and drives you to Sanur Harbor in East Denpasar.

  2. 2

    Fast boat to Nusa Penida

    A 30 to 40 minute crossing to Sampalan or Toyapakeh Harbor. We handle the tickets and your luggage at both ends.

  3. 3

    Up to the resort

    Our driver meets you at the harbor. The check-in desk is 15 to 20 minutes up the hill.
