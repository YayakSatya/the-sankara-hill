Radha, the Beloved

Devotion, love, and spiritual connection

Radha (Sanskrit: राधा) is traditionally known as the beloved of Krishna, symbolizing devotion, love, and spiritual connection. The name represents a sanctuary of well-being where weary souls can pause, reconnect, and find a deeper sense of peace, high above the island and embraced by the serenity of Nusa Penida.

A tranquil wellness sanctuary offering holistic treatments inspired by Balinese healing traditions. Guided by experienced therapists, guests are invited to restore balance and relaxation in a serene hilltop setting.

Rituals from the Ridge

Holistic treatments inspired by Balinese healing traditions, guided by experienced therapists.

Massages

  • Sky-Ridge Balinese Massage 60 or 90 min Signature

    Long strokes, palm pressure and warm coconut oil, taken in an open pavilion facing the strait.

  • Warm Stone Therapy 90 min

    Heated volcanic stones placed along the spine to ease deep muscular tension.

  • Island Foot Reflexology 45 min

    Pressure-point work on the feet and lower legs, ideal after a day of cliff walks.

  • Four-Hands Harmony 60 min

    Two therapists moving in rhythm, blending Balinese and Swedish techniques.

Body and Face

  • Sea Salt and Frangipani Polish 60 min

    Penida sea salt and frangipani petals, followed by a rain shower and a hydrating body milk.

  • Boreh Warming Wrap 75 min

    A traditional Balinese paste of ginger, clove and rice powder to warm and detoxify.

  • Hilltop Glow Facial 60 min

    Cleanse, exfoliate and a cooling cucumber mask for sun-tired skin.

  • Cream Bath Hair Ritual 45 min

    Avocado and coconut cream scalp massage with a steam towel finish.
