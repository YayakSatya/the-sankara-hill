Puñña Restaurant

Fullness, completeness, quiet abundance

Puñña (Pali: पुञ्ञ) evokes fullness, completeness, abundance, and fulfillment. Closely associated with the imagery of the full moon, it reflects a sense of wholeness and quiet abundance. The name draws inspiration from the luminous full moon (Puññacanda) that illuminates the limestone hills and surrounding ocean of Nusa Penida.

Designed for refined all-day dining overlooking the picturesque hills and ocean of Nusa Penida, Puñña Restaurant offers elegant indoor and alfresco seating options. The restaurant presents a thoughtfully curated selection of Indonesian and international Mediterranean cuisines, crafted from fresh, locally sourced ingredients to deliver a memorable culinary experience from breakfast through dinner.

Sukha Pool & Sunset Deck

A State of Ease

Sip on an array of refreshing beverages and handcrafted cocktails while basking in the island sun or unwinding after a day of exploration. Adjacent to Puñña Restaurant and the infinity pool, Sukha Pool & Deck offers a relaxed yet sophisticated setting overlooking the ocean horizon, perfect for leisurely afternoons and captivating sunsets.

Sukha (Pali: सुख) means happiness, comfort, ease, and well-being, the opposite of Dukkha, or suffering. The name captures the essence of the experience: swim, relax, sip, take in the view, and watch the sunset. A simple state of being where the body feels light and the mind can simply be at ease.
