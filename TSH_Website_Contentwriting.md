## ABOUT

### THE SANKARA
**A Name That Calls the Soul Home**

The name **Sankara** is rooted in Sanskrit, the ancient language of India and a profound influence on the spiritual traditions of Hinduism and Buddhism. Traditionally associated with auspiciousness, benevolence, and positive transformation, *Sankara* evokes the idea of one who brings forth happiness and well-being.

The name also recalls the sacred sound of the **conch shell trumpet**, traditionally used in Hindu ceremonies and described in the *Mahabharata* as a powerful call marking significant moments. Its resonant sound continues to carry spiritual meaning, symbolising purity, sanctity, and a connection to the divine.

At The Sankara, this symbolism is reinterpreted as a gentle call to return to what matters most — to nature, to cultural roots, and to the stillness within.

It is a journey back to the source: a place where the rhythm of nature, the richness of tradition, and the beauty of the island come together to inspire peace, balance, and a renewed sense of connection.

**The Sankara — where every journey brings you closer to the source.**

**The Altitude's Serenade**

Perched upon the tranquil hills of Nusa Penida, The Sankara Hill Penida is a refined island sanctuary where elevated comfort meets sweeping ocean panoramas. An infinity pool, curated dining experiences, wellness facilities, and attentive hospitality complete the experience, inviting guests to slow down, reconnect, and embrace the beauty and tranquillity of Nusa Penida.

At The Sankara Hill Penida, the island unfolds from a different perspective — above, beyond, and at your own pace.

# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

## Getting to The Sankara Hill Penida

**How can I get to The Sankara Hill Penida?**
The Sankara Hill Penida is located in Ped, Nusa Penida, Bali. Guests can reach the island by fast boat from Bali's main harbours, including Sanur, Kusamba, or Padang Bai.

For a seamless journey, our Reservation Team can assist in arranging your fast boat tickets and transportation from the harbour to the resort. We recommend arranging your transfer in advance, especially during peak travel periods.

**Can you arrange a fast boat transfer and pick-up from the harbour?**
Yes. Our Reservation Team can assist with fast boat arrangements and transportation from the Nusa Penida harbour to The Sankara Hill Penida.

Simply let us know your preferred travel date and departure point, and our team will assist with the available options.

**Do you provide airport transfers?**
We do not operate a direct airport shuttle. However, we can assist in arranging transportation from Ngurah Rai International Airport to the fast boat harbour in Bali, followed by your boat transfer to Nusa Penida.

Please contact our Reservation Team in advance to arrange your journey.

**How long does it take to reach The Sankara Hill Penida from Bali?**
The fast boat journey from Bali to Nusa Penida generally takes around 30–60 minutes, depending on the departure harbour, sea conditions, and boat operator.

From the harbour in Nusa Penida, the journey to The Sankara Hill Penida generally takes around 15–30 minutes by car, depending on the arrival harbour and traffic conditions.

## Accommodation & Stay

**What time is check-in and check-out?**
Check-in is from 2:00 PM, while check-out is by 12:00 PM.

**Is early check-in available?**
Early check-in is subject to availability on the day of arrival.

If you arrive early, our team will be happy to assist with your luggage while you wait for your room to be ready.

**Is late check-out available?**
Late check-out is subject to availability and may be subject to an additional charge depending on the requested departure time.

Please contact our Front Office Team during your stay for assistance.

**Is Wi-Fi available at the resort?**
Yes. Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the resort, including guest rooms and public areas.

**Is there a deposit required upon check-in?**
Yes. A security deposit of IDR 500,000 is required upon arrival and can be settled by cash or debit/credit card.

The deposit may be used toward incidental expenses during your stay. If there are no outstanding charges upon check-out, the remaining deposit will be returned to you.

**Can I request a specific room or villa?**
Guests may submit a room or villa preference when making a reservation. Specific room numbers or locations are subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed.

Our Reservation Team will do their best to accommodate your request.

**Are extra beds available?**
Yes. Extra single beds are available upon request and are subject to availability. Availability is limited and may vary depending on the accommodation category.

Additional charges may apply. Please contact our Reservation Team in advance to check availability and the applicable rate for your selected room or villa.

**Are baby cots available?**
Baby cots are available upon request and are subject to availability.

We recommend requesting a baby cot when making your reservation.

**Is the resort suitable for families with children?**
Children are welcome at The Sankara Hill Penida. Some room categories and facilities may have specific occupancy conditions.

Please contact our Reservation Team with your children's ages so we can recommend the most suitable accommodation.

**Do you allow pets?**
No. Pets are not permitted at The Sankara Hill Penida.

**Does the resort have accessible facilities?**
The resort does not currently provide dedicated accessible facilities. Due to the property's hilltop setting and multi-level layout, guests with mobility requirements are encouraged to contact our Reservation Team before booking so we can advise on the most suitable accommodation and access arrangements.

## Resort & Destination

**Is The Sankara Hill Penida a beachfront resort?**
No. The Sankara Hill Penida is a hilltop resort, rather than a beachfront property.

Its elevated setting offers a sense of privacy and tranquillity, with views across the surrounding landscape and ocean.

**How far is The Sankara Hill Penida from the beach?**
The Sankara Hill Penida is located away from the beachfront, with several beaches accessible by car from the resort.

Our team will be happy to assist in arranging transportation and recommending beaches or other destinations during your stay.

**Can the resort arrange activities and experiences in Nusa Penida?**
Yes. Our team can help arrange selected experiences and transportation around Nusa Penida, depending on availability.

We recommend speaking with our team before your arrival so your preferred activities can be arranged in advance.

**Is The Sankara Hill Penida suitable for honeymooners?**
The Sankara Hill Penida offers a tranquil hilltop setting, private pool villas, ocean views, spa experiences, and intimate dining options, making it an ideal setting for a romantic island escape.

Our team can also assist with special arrangements for honeymoon and romantic stays.

**Can I arrange special occasions at the resort?**
Yes. Our team can assist with selected arrangements for special occasions such as birthdays, anniversaries, honeymoons, and romantic celebrations.

Please contact our Reservation Team in advance so we can discuss the available arrangements and additional charges.

**What should I know before travelling to Nusa Penida?**
Nusa Penida is an island destination with a naturally hilly landscape. Travel times may vary depending on road conditions, traffic, harbour schedules, and sea conditions.

We recommend allowing sufficient travel time between your boat arrival, resort transfer, and onward activities. Our Reservation Team will be happy to assist in planning your journey.

**How can I make a reservation?**
You can make a reservation through our official booking channels or contact our Reservation Team directly.

For assistance with accommodation, transfers, special requests, or stay arrangements, please contact us before your arrival.
